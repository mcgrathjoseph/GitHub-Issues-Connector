Key Rule Clarified
==================
Browsers are suitable only for testing GET endpoints.
POST endpoints must be tested using tools such as:
- PowerShell (Invoke-RestMethod)
- curl
- Postman or equivalent REST clients

Attempting to access POST endpoints through a browser URL bar will always result in a 404 response.
