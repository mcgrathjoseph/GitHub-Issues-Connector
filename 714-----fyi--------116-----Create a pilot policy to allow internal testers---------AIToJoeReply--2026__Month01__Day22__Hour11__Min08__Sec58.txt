Summary of Key Points:
- You can hand the ZIP to someone with higher Teams admin rights.
- They upload it under Teams > Apps > Manage your apps > Upload an app.
- This sideloads it for testing or they can publish org-wide.

Full User Question:
What would I do with the zip if I wanted to deploy to someone who had more rights than me?

Full Reply Context:
Give them appPackage.local.zip. They open the Teams client, go to Apps > Manage your apps > Upload an app > Upload a custom app. This adds your agent for testing. Admins can also publish to the org via Admin Center.
